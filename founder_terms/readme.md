# Founder Terms

A comprehensive Anki deck covering essential startup and founder terminology across fundraising, hardware development, business metrics, legal concepts, and strategy.

## Overview

This deck contains **83 founder terms** with in-depth definitions designed to provide complete understanding without requiring additional research.

## Categories Covered

- **Fundraising & VC Terms** (32 cards) - Term sheets, valuations, SAFEs, convertible notes, liquidation preferences, anti-dilution, board seats, and investment structures
- **Hardware-Specific Terms** (17 cards) - BOM, NRE, MOQ, tooling, DFM, EVT/DVT/PVT, contract manufacturing, COGS, and supply chain
- **Business & Financial Terms** (16 cards) - ARR/MRR, unit economics, CAC, LTV, churn, TAM/SAM/SOM, margins, P&L, cash flow, and revenue models
- **Legal & Governance Terms** (9 cards) - IP, NDA, LOI, MOU, fiduciary duty, MAC, representations & warranties, and indemnification
- **Startup Strategy Terms** (9 cards) - Product-market fit, GTM, beachhead market, moat, pivot, traction, North Star metric, and MVP

## Format

- **Front of card:** Term name
- **Back of card:** Detailed definition with examples, calculations, and practical context
- **Acronyms:** Formatted with full name followed by explanation
  - Example: "SAFE" → "Simple Agreement for Future Equity" + detailed explanation

## Target Audience

- Startup founders and co-founders
- Early employees at startups
- Aspiring entrepreneurs
- Investors and venture capitalists
- Anyone working in the startup ecosystem
